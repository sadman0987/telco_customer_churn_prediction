Project overview

This report applies data analytics and predictive modelling to the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset to identify the key drivers of customer churn in the telecommunications sector. It combines linear regression, logistic regression, hypothesis testing, and a simulated A/B test to turn data-driven insights into actionable customer retention strategies — shifting the business from reacting to churn to preventing it.

Month-to-month risk

Contract type is the strongest single predictor — month-to-month customers churn at a far higher rate than those on annual contracts.

Pricing pressure

A t-test (p < 0.05) confirms churned customers pay significantly higher monthly charges than retained ones.

Early tenure is critical

Churn risk concentrates in a customer's first year, pointing to a window for early intervention.

Retention offers work

Simulated A/B testing shows high-risk customers who receive a targeted offer churn less than a control group.

Methods applied

  • 01

    Linear regression

    Forecasts how monthly charges relate to customer tenure, exposing revenue patterns as customers stay longer.

  • 02

    Logistic regression

    Classifies churn risk using contract type, tenure, and monthly charges as predictors.

  • 03

    Hypothesis testing

    An independent samples t-test verifies whether pricing differs significantly between churned and retained customers.

  • 04

    A/B testing

    A simulated retention experiment measures whether targeted offers reduce churn among high-risk customers.
